Data Requests

How to make a request

Email support@gamayunoutsourcing.com with the subject line: Plastirot Data Request.

What to include

  • Your request type: access, correction, deletion, objection, or general privacy question.
  • The email address you used to contact support, if relevant.
  • Google Play order number if your request concerns a purchase.
  • Any details needed to identify the issue without sending unnecessary personal information.

Local game data

Some game data may exist only on your device, such as settings, saves, and local purchase flags. You can remove local app data through Android system settings by clearing app storage or uninstalling the game.
