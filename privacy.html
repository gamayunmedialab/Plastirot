Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2026

1. Who we are

Plastirot is developed and published by Gamayun Outsourcing Limited. For privacy and support enquiries, contact: gamayun.medialab@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Plastirot may handle data in relation to the game, support requests, Google Play purchases, advertising, diagnostics, and store operation.

3. Data handled by third-party services

Depending on the release build and enabled SDKs, Plastirot may use third-party services such as Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, crash reporting, analytics, or platform services. These services may process data such as advertising identifiers, device identifiers, approximate usage data, diagnostics, crash data, purchase status, and fraud-prevention signals.

The exact data shown in the Google Play Data Safety section must match the SDKs included in the active release build.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases in the Google Play version are handled by Google Play Billing. Plastirot may store purchase entitlement state locally, such as whether the ad-free purchase is active, so the game can apply the correct behaviour.

5. Advertising

Plastirot may show rewarded ads and, if enabled, interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are optional. Players who own the ad-free purchase should not receive forced ads where ad-free removal is supported.

6. Support messages

If you contact support, we may process the information you provide, such as your email address, message content, device details you choose to send, purchase order number, screenshots, and bug descriptions. This is used to answer your request, investigate bugs, and handle purchase or privacy issues.

7. Data retention

Support correspondence may be retained for as long as needed to handle the request and maintain records. Local game settings and purchase flags may remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the game.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal data. To make a request, email gamayun.medialab@gmail.com with the subject line “Plastirot Data Request”.

9. Children

Plastirot is not designed for children. If the Google Play listing sets a specific age rating, that rating controls store presentation and availability.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as the game, SDK configuration, advertising, purchases, or support systems change.
