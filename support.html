Bug reports

Please include, where possible:

  • Game version/build number.
  • Your device model and Android version.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • Screenshots or short video if useful.

Purchase issues

If a Google Play purchase does not appear in-game, restart the game first. If the issue remains, contact support and include your Google Play order number.
